Wetterbank® is a bench that starts conversations between strangers, by helping them file complaints about the weather.

A design fiction asking how autonomous systems will shape our encounters in public space, as they move from our homes onto our streets.

Wetterbank® is a design fiction and an ongoing research project. The bench does not exist. Yet.

How it adds up.

Three things everyone at a German tram stop already knows.

  1. The weather is the safest way to start a conversation.
  2. +The tram is late. Again.
  3. +Nobody at the stop says a word.
  4. =A bench that speaks first.

Wetterbank® started as a design fiction on my portfolio. This site is where it continues.

Three modes, one step at a time.

Names · Mode 3

It drops the weather and asks for a name.

The question.

How do interactive and autonomous systems shape social encounters in public space?

  1. Want. Do people even want to talk, to each other, or to a bench?
  2. Help. Can a bench help an encounter, or does it get in the way?
  3. Future. How will we react when objects like this become normal, and should they?

Wetterbank® doesn’t exist yet. Should it?
